Vijay's Journey from Cinema to Politics Vijay's journey from a leading actor to a political leader is a remarkable one. Born on June 22, 1974, in Chennai, Tamil Nadu, Vijay began his career as a child artist and made his acting debut in 1992. He quickly rose to fame with films like "Poove Unakkaga" and "Kadhalukku Mariyadhai," becoming one of the highest-paid actors in India. His transition into politics began with his founding of the Tamilaga Vettri Kazhagam (TVK) in 2026, where he has emerged as a major political force. Vijay's political party, TVK, has secured a historic victory in the 2026 Tamil Nadu Assembly elections, making him the Chief Minister of Tamil Nadu. His political career is marked by a focus on governance, welfare, and youth outreach, reflecting his commitment to addressing social issues in Tamil Nadu.
